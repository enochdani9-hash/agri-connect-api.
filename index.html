Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying securely, boosting your sales, and using AgromartDirect.

📦 Selling & Posting

Is it free to sell on AgromartDirect?

Yes! You can post up to 3 standard listings completely free of charge. Your ads will remain active for 30 days. We only charge for optional visibility boosts and our Agromart Pro monthly subscription.

How do I post a Buyer Tender (RFQ)?

Simply click the "📢 Post Buy Request" button at the top of your screen. State what you need, your target price, and town. Local Ghanaian farmers will view your tender and contact you directly via WhatsApp to supply.

🛡️ Trust & Safety

How do I buy safely?

Always inspect the farm produce before finalizing payment. We highly recommend utilizing the MoMo Safe-Trade Protection protocol—meet the farmer, confirm the product quality matches the listing, and then release your Mobile Money payment.

What does the "Verified Seller" badge mean?

Farmers with a Verified Seller badge have completed our manual background check process. This helps build trust within the community, though standard marketplace precautions should always be taken.
