About AgriConnect

AgriConnect was built with a simple but powerful mission: to bridge the gap between hardworking farmers and eager buyers in Accra.

For too long, the agricultural supply chain has been slowed down by middlemen, driving up costs for consumers and cutting into the profits of local farmers. We believe that technology can solve this.

By providing a completely free, zero-commission digital marketplace, AgriConnect empowers poultry farmers, vegetable growers, and livestock rearers to showcase their fresh produce directly to the community. Whether you are looking for a single crate of eggs, day-old chicks for your own farm, or bulk vegetables for a restaurant, our platform connects you instantly via WhatsApp or a direct phone call.

🌱 Our Vision

To become Ghana's most trusted digital farmers' market, ensuring food security, increasing agricultural profitability, and delivering farm-fresh quality directly to every household.
